# Peacock anatomy references — milestone 4.5

- [Closeup of an Indian Blue Peacock's head](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Closeup_of_an_Indian_Blue_Peacock%27s_head.jpg), photograph by Julie Slama / Zaui, April 2004, Utah's Hogle Zoo. [Local unchanged original](peacock-head-photo.jpg). Licensed [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) (also GFDL). Downloaded and visually inspected 2026-09-06. Used to study the small lateral eye, separate pale upper/lower facial markings, taper and nostril of the horn bill, bare slender crest shafts with small terminal barbs, dense blue neck feather direction, and the green shoulder plumage visible behind the neck.
- [Northern India 2014, Birding for Life](https://birdingforlife.com/2014/12/29/northern-india-2014-part-1-of-2/), peacock head/neck profile encountered during photographic reference search; linked for further anatomical comparison, not copied into the app.
- [Original supplied art reference](peacock.png), retained for monumental fan hierarchy, blue/green/antique-gold palette and sculptural presentation. It is art direction rather than a photographic anatomical source.

The photographs are reference material only. No photograph or reference raster is imported by application source or mapped onto the sculpture. The bird, plumage, markings and bill remain procedural geometry and shading.
